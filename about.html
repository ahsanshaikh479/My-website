WHO WE ARE

Welcome to APSIS Hyderabad

Army Public School for International Studies, Hyderabad is committed to providing students with a balanced educational experience in a caring and disciplined environment.

We seek to support academic achievement while giving students opportunities to participate in sports, co-curricular activities, creative work and community experiences.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, capable and responsible learners prepared to contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To provide purposeful learning experiences that develop knowledge, skills, values and character.

Our Values

Respect, integrity, discipline, responsibility, teamwork and a commitment to continuous learning.

PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE

A Message from the Principal

Welcome to Army Public School for International Studies, Hyderabad. Education is a shared journey involving students, teachers, parents and the wider community.

Our aim is to create an environment where students feel encouraged to learn, ask questions, participate actively and develop confidence. We value both academic progress and the personal qualities that help young people become responsible members of society.

We look forward to working together to support every student's learning journey.

Principal
